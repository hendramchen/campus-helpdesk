# Business Requirements

## BR-001: Submit Ticket

A student can submit a helpdesk ticket.

Required information:

- Subject
- Description
- Category
- Priority

## BR-002: View Tickets

Users can view ticket listings.

## BR-003: View Ticket Details

Users can view the details of an individual ticket.

## BR-004: Update Ticket Status

Authorized IT support users can update
the status of a ticket.
